## Pre-work · Hypothesis check
- **Role , who you are solving for (from M2):** A fleet dispatcher at a mid-size 3PL.
- **Goal , what this user is ultimately trying to achieve:** To reassign routes and have drivers act on the change right away.
- **Friction / moment of misery , the specific pain blocking their goal:** Further analysis will confirm that when a dispatcher reassigns a route, the driver does not see the change in the app for ten to fifteen minutes, and by then he has already driven the wrong way. - **Current workaround , the external tool or manual process they rely on (M2):** Whatsapp
- **Problem Hook , your one-sentence framing of the business crisis (M1):** We must stop losing real time fleet coordination because a route reassignment takes ten to fifteen minutes to reach the driver with no notification, so dispatchers have moved coordination into a WhatsApp group instead of the system, leaving RouteLogic blind to the route changes it should be tracking.
- **Value Proposition , the outcome your initiative promised to deliver (M1):** For the dispatcher who needs a route reassignment to reach the driver immediately, we will deliver the change to the driver's app in real time with a push notification, so that she can stop relying on a WhatsApp group to coordinate the fleet, because every reassignment that lags ten to fifteen minutes sends a driver the wrong way and keeps coordination happening outside the system.
