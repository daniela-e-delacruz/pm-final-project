## Pre-work · Hypothesis check
- **Role , who you are solving for (from M2):** A real-time fleet coordinator who keeps daily operations running and is responsible for logging compliance checks during her shift.
- **Goal , what this user is ultimately trying to achieve:** To keep compliance records up to date in RouteLogic without slowing down the operations she coordinates.
- **Friction / moment of misery , the specific pain blocking their goal:** When she logs a compliance check during her shift, she has to go through four screens while other operational tasks are waiting. By the fourth screen, time pressure pushes her out of RouteLogic, and she switches to a spreadsheet to load the checks later. As a result, compliance records reach RouteLogic late, and the executive team loses the real-time view it needs to make decisions.
- **Current workaround , the external tool or manual process they rely on (M2):** A spreadsheet kept outside RouteLogic as a shadow compliance log. She abandons the flow at the fourth screen, records the check in the spreadsheet to get back to operations, and later transfers the checks into RouteLogic. The work is done twice and RouteLogic stays out of date in between.
- **Problem Hook , your one-sentence framing of the business crisis (M1):** We must stop the lack of engagement of real-time fleet coordinators because logging compliance checks is such an administrative burden that they drop the system by the fourth screen and switch to spreadsheets, delaying compliance records and the real-time tracking that executives need.
- **Value Proposition , the outcome your initiative promised to deliver (M1):** For real-time fleet coordinators, we will allow them to register compliance checks within RouteLogic, keeping advanced enterprise features available but out of their way, because this process is an administrative burden that affects real-time operations and the statistics the executive team uses to make decisions now, giving competitors an opportunity to win our biggest account.
