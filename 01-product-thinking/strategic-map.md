## Responses
- **Write one feature from the map that, if an engineer asked “why is this a priority for our 12-week roadmap?”, you honestly could not give a strategic answer for.:** Weekly charts and trends. It's a "nice to have": it shows users their past behavior, but it doesn't solve the core job of building the drinking habit day to day, and nothing in the brief tells me users are asking for it. If an engineer asked why it should be in the next 12 weeks, I couldn't give a strategic answer.
- **Write one feature that looks “correct” for the app but has zero strategic weight, you have no idea if it helps the company make money or whether a single user actually asked for it.:** Health app sync. It looks right for a wellness app, but the AI proposed it without connecting it to any problem or user we identified. I don't know if a single user asked for it or if it would help the company make money.
- **Compare V3 to your Step 1 baseline. What changed, and is the output objectively better, did the AI get smarter, or did your added PM knowledge (JTBD, effort/impact, retention) force it to be useful?:** The baseline was a basic mindmap with four generic branches: logging, reminders, goals and personalization. V3 was much more useful because my prompt forced the structure: jobs-to-be-done, features ranked by effort and impact, monetization, retention risks and strategic tensions. The AI didn't get smarter, my PM framing did the work. But it also filled gaps with information that wasn't based on data, since it doesn't have any, so the output looks more confident than the evidence behind it.
- **Key takeaway: how does this prove the PM’s role is to think while the AI’s role is to execute?:** The AI organized the ideas fast, but it couldn't decide. It flagged real tensions, like whether the app should rely on reminders or on passive tracking, and whether to put the features that build the habit behind a paywall, but choosing between them is a strategic decision based on users and business goals. I also had to filter features like health app sync that sounded right but had no evidence behind them. The AI executes the structure; the PM decides what matters and why.
