# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** The gap between RouteLogic's buyers, the exec team that makes the purchasing decisions and the actual users (95% of our users) increased as the product became more complex. As a result, we are already seeing our biggest account actively exploring other options. So much so that the customer describes the issue as “administrative burden”. Internal data shows a sharp decline in usage among the people responsible for real-time fleet coordination. We are losing ground as execs realize their teams reject the tool. Expansion stalls first, then renewals come up with no advocacy on our behalf and in 12 months our most important accounts terminate their contracts and show a new path for new deals in the segment to go to competitors as the best option. We will lose our value as a fast solution to operational problems to become a reporting back office. Being able to assist the complexity of execs' needs is not the problem, the problem is how it’s getting in the way of daily users. RouteLogic stops being the default choice for new deals in this segment, competitors become the new standard, and our position shifts from operational leader to a legacy reporting tool used only by accounts too locked-in to switch
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Further analysis will confirm that when the Operator needs to confirm why a shipment is stuck in customs on a Wednesday at 4:45pm and customs closes at 5pm, she goes into the system across 5 screens looking for the shipping number to check the shipment's status directly on the customs website. When she gets to the 3rd screen without finding it, she gives up and calls customs directly instead.The issue is resolved, but she's lost 30 minutes that could have gone toward actually fixing it, instead of just tracking it down.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must stop losing operational time on shipments stuck in customs because the operator can't find the shipment's status fast enough across so many screens that she gives up and calls customs directly instead, and every one of those recoveries goes unrecorded, leaving execs with zero data on how often the system is failing the people who keep freight moving.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For the operator that needs to find the tracking number fast, we will provide a way to resolve customs issues in one screen so she can avoid the loss of 30 extra minutes and the limiting of the strategic information in the system. Because every minute she spends searching instead of solving is time the problem goes unresolved, unrecorded, and visibility keeps getting lost.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** High-stakes. This is not a usability annoyance: our largest enterprise account is already trialing a leaner competitor, and the executive reporting the C-suite buys us for is missing crucial information making it useless. The risk is losing both the account and the role we play in the market, not a few hours of user friction.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** This is a systemic problem, not an edge case: internal data shows a sharp decline in usage among the people responsible for real-time fleet coordination (the very users who make up 95% of RouteLogic's user base), meaning this kind of unresolved, unrecorded workaround is happening across the board, not just for this operator on this shipment. It's chipping away at the real-time visibility that used to be RouteLogic's core value in the market.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** It removes it rather than dressing it up. A faster search across the same 5 screens would still leave her digging for the tracking number under time pressure. The fix isn't speed, it's putting everything she needs in one place so she never has to leave the system to call customs. The test is concrete: if operators are still calling customs directly in six months, it didn't work.
