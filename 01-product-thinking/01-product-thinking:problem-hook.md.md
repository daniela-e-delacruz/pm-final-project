# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** The gap between RouteLogic's buyers, the exec team that makes the purchasing decisions and the actual users (95% of our users) increased as the product became more complex. As a result, we are already seeing our biggest account actively exploring other options. So much so that the customer describes the issue as “administrative burden”. Internal data shows a sharp decline in usage among the people responsible for real-time fleet coordination. We are losing ground as execs realize their teams reject the tool. Expansion stalls first, then renewals come up with no advocacy on our behalf and in 12 months our most important accounts terminate their contracts and show a new path for new deals in the segment to go to competitors as the best option. We will lose our value as a fast solution to operational problems to become a reporting back office. Being able to assist the complexity of execs' needs is not the problem, the problem is how it’s getting in the way of daily users. RouteLogic stops being the default choice for new deals in this segment, competitors become the new standard, and our position shifts from operational leader to a legacy reporting tool used only by accounts too locked-in to switch
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Further analysis will confirm that when a dispatcher reassigns a route, the driver does not see the change in the app for ten to fifteen minutes, and by then he has already driven the wrong way. There is no push notification when a route changes, so the dispatcher has stopped relying on RouteLogic for this and keeps a WhatsApp group as the real system instead.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must stop losing real time fleet coordination because a route reassignment takes ten to fifteen minutes to reach the driver with no notification, so dispatchers have moved coordination into a WhatsApp group instead of the system, leaving RouteLogic blind to the route changes it should be tracking.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For the dispatcher who needs a route reassignment to reach the driver immediately, we will deliver the change to the driver's app in real time with a push notification, so that she can stop relying on a WhatsApp group to coordinate the fleet, because every reassignment that lags ten to fifteen minutes sends a driver the wrong way and keeps coordination happening outside the system.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** High stakes. This is not a usability annoyance: an ops manager at an enterprise account said RouteLogic does everything, which is the problem, since frontline people use maybe five percent of it and cannot find that five percent, and they are evaluating a leaner competitor that just does routing well. A regional manager confirmed the daily driver experience is dragging down adoption and renewal is at risk. The risk is losing the account and the role we play in the market, not a few hours of user friction.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** This is a systemic problem, not an edge case. Diego texts his dispatcher instead of marking a stop delivered in the app. The dispatcher keeps a WhatsApp group as the real system for reassignments. Five of seven drivers in a focus group keep a paper manifest in case the app fails. Each of these is a different symptom of the same problem: people are leaving the system because it cannot keep up with real time coordination, and that is chipping away at the real time visibility that used to be RouteLogic's core value in the market.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** It removes it rather than dressing it up. A notification alone would not fix this if the underlying delay stays the same. The fix delivers the reassignment to the driver in real time, which is what actually replaces the need for a WhatsApp group. The test is concrete: if dispatchers are still coordinating reassignments over WhatsApp in six months, it did not work.
