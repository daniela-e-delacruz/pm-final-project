# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If the company doesn't keep its largest account, in the next 12 months leaner competitors will become the new status quo. The 95% of users who rely on the product daily for time sensitive work, slowed down by what our largest account called an "administrative burden", will start looking for other solutions. This opens the door to our lean competitors, and as usage among these operators keeps declining, RouteLogic will lose its position as the fastest tool on the market.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** At 5:45, fifteen minutes before her shift ends, a real-time fleet coordinator still has to log the day's compliance checks in RouteLogic. Each check walks her through screens built for global shipping compliance, not for a regional delivery route. By the fourth screen of the first check, she drops the system and records the rest in a spreadsheet, planning to enter them all on one day at the end of the month. The checks eventually get logged, but for up to 30 days the company has no live record of its compliance status, and the executive reports built on that data are already outdated when they are read.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must stop the lack of engagement of real-time fleet coordinators because logging compliance checks is such an administrative burden that they drop the system by the 4th screen and switch to spreadsheets, delaying compliance records and the real-time tracking that executives need.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For real-time fleet coordinators, we will allow them to register compliance checks within RouteLogic, keeping advanced enterprise features available but out of their way, because this process is an administrative burden that affects real-time operations and the statistics the executive team uses to make decisions now, giving competitors an opportunity to win our biggest account.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** Yes, it is a high-stakes threat. Our largest account is already trialing a leaner competitor, and daily usage among coordinators is declining.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** It points to a systemic problem: logging compliance checks is a daily task for real-time fleet coordinators, and the friction comes from how the product is designed, not from a rare situation. This still needs to be validated with research and usage data.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** It removes the obstacle at the exact moment coordinators drop the system: if they can log compliance checks within RouteLogic, they no longer switch to spreadsheets, and the data stays in real time.
