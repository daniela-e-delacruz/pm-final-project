# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** RouteLogic Velocity (B2B)

## Strategic crisis
The gap between RouteLogic's buyers, the exec team that makes the purchasing decisions and the actual users (95% of our users) increased as the product became more complex. As a result, we are already seeing our biggest account actively exploring other options. So much so that the customer describes the issue as “administrative burden”. Internal data shows a sharp decline in usage among the people responsible for real-time fleet coordination. 
We are losing ground as execs realize their teams reject the tool. Expansion stalls first, then renewals come up with no advocacy on our behalf and in 12 months our most important accounts terminate their contracts and show a new path for new deals in the segment to go to competitors as the best option. 
We will lose our value as a fast solution to operational problems to become a reporting back office. 
Being able to assist the complexity of execs' needs is not the problem, the problem is how it’s getting in the way of daily users.

## Moment of misery
Further analysis will confirm that when the dispatcher needs to ship several international packages to a high-risk zone on a Wednesday at 4:45pm and customs closes at 5pm, product details, material compositions and shipping justifications need to be uploaded into the system across several screens in order to give the necessary information for statistics and an exec analysis. When the dispatcher gets to the 3rd form, she decides to open a spreadsheet to log the most relevant data so she can go back to the system and upload it at the end of the month. The packages get shipped on time. In this case, the data loses value for the decision making process given that it is uploaded from memory in a single day.

## Problem hook
We must stop losing key accounts because the dispatcher has to upload information across so many screens to clear a shipment that she leaves the system for a spreadsheet she fills from memory, and uploads it at the end of the month - and the data becomes less valuable for the same analysis it’s intended to improve.

## Value proposition
For Dispatcher who has to ship a lot of packages in a short period of time., we will Capture the compliance records from the dispatch itself, so the data lands in real time and the spreadsheet stops being where the operation lives.  because Key accounts are considering terminating their contracts as the user moves away from the system every month and the spreadsheet becomes more relevant..

## Cold-read self-review
The strongest point of this analysis is the irony. The fields that the exec team requested are the ones pushing the data out of the system. So the fix doesn’t ask the leadership to lose visibility, it improves it. 

The weakest part is the example. We need to look into the data to see the percentage of international shipments and if this is also happening in other cases.
